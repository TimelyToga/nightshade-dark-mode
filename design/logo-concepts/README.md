# Eclipse Leaf concepts

Three generated explorations of the Eclipse Leaf direction. Each source PNG is 1254 × 1254 with transparency and is intended for evaluation, not yet wired into the extension manifest.

| Flat geometric | Bold badge | Soft dimensional |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ![Flat geometric Eclipse Leaf](eclipse-leaf-flat.png) | ![Bold badge Eclipse Leaf](eclipse-leaf-badge.png) | ![Soft dimensional Eclipse Leaf](eclipse-leaf-dimensional.png) |

The shipping mark should be selected after checking the candidates at Chrome's 16 px and 32 px toolbar sizes. The flat or badge direction will generally survive that reduction better than the dimensional version.

## Sci-fi corona explorations

| Solar corona + beam | Reactor ring + beam | Volumetric beam |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ![Eclipse Leaf with a dramatic plasma corona and diagonal beam](eclipse-leaf-corona-beam.png) | ![Eclipse Leaf as a segmented energy reactor with a horizontal beam](eclipse-leaf-reactor-beam.png) | ![Eclipse Leaf with a coronal rim and wide volumetric beam](eclipse-leaf-volumetric-beam.png) |

The solar-corona variant preserves the strongest leaf silhouette at 16 px. The reactor variant is the most overtly futuristic but least botanical, while the volumetric variant makes the light beam most obvious.
